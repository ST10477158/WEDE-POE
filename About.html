About Us

History of Organisation: ⦁ Kopanong Community Centre is a Non-Profit Organisation that was formed in 2020. It was formed in order to help and provide support for local community children. It was formed by community leaders, because the majority of community children were suffering from hunger. The centre started with a feeding program and also came up with activities. Kopanong Community centre is now feeding more than 70 children in the Community, also helping them with school work. It also partnered with local schools and some of the local businesses in the Community. Our journey is proof that when a community comes together, we can change lives and build a brighter future for our children. .

Mission of Organisation: Our mission is to take children off the street preventing high rates of crime in the community. Provide support and food so that they cannot sleep with empty stomach, and also assist them with school work.

Vision of Organisation: Our vision is to build an environment where children will access food and participate in certain activities that will help them engage with others, also to help them reach their full potential.
